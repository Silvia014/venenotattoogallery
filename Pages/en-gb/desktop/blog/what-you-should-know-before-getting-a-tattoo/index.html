What you should know before getting a tattoo: advice, risks and aftercare

5-minute read

Find out about risks, aftercare and pricing before getting a tattoo.

Getting a tattoo is a personal decision that can become an incredible experience when it is approached with planning and carried out in a professional studio.

Before booking your appointment, there are several important things you should know. Understanding the process can help you make an informed decision and enjoy the experience from the very beginning.

Choose your studio and tattoo artist carefully

One of the most important steps is choosing a studio that follows appropriate hygiene procedures and works with experienced professionals.

It is also a good idea to look through an artist's previous work and make sure their style matches the result you are looking for.

Think about the design and placement

The design and its placement are closely connected. Size, line work, level of detail and the shape of the area can all have a significant influence on the final result.

  • Choose a design you genuinely connect with.
  • Consider the size needed to preserve its details.
  • Ask your tattoo artist about the best placement.
  • Consider how the tattoo may change over time.

Hygiene and safety

Hygiene is essential throughout the entire process. A professional studio should work with appropriate equipment and maintain clean conditions that help reduce the risks associated with tattooing.

A good tattoo starts long before the needle touches the skin.

Does getting a tattoo hurt?

The sensation varies from person to person and also depends on the area being tattooed. Some parts of the body can be more sensitive than others, so everyone's experience can be different.

Talking to your tattoo artist before the session can help you understand what to expect and prepare for your appointment.

Looking after your tattoo

Once the tattoo is finished, an equally important part begins: allowing your skin to recover properly.

  • Follow the specific instructions given by your tattoo artist.
  • Keep the area clean according to the aftercare instructions.
  • Avoid touching or scratching the area while it heals.
  • Protect the tattoo from unnecessary sun exposure.

How much does a tattoo cost?

The price of a tattoo can vary depending on its size, level of detail, placement, style and the time required to complete it.

For this reason, the best way to find out the price of a specific project is to explain your idea and speak directly with the studio.

A decision worth thinking about

A tattoo can become part of your body for many years. Taking the time to choose the right design, studio and professional can make a significant difference to both the experience and the final result.
